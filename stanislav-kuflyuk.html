Biography

Ukrainian baritone Stanislav Kuflyuk is recognized for his interpretations of the Verdi, Tchaikovsky, and Italian lyric-dramatic repertoire, appearing regularly with leading opera houses and festivals across Europe.

Read More

Highlights of his recent seasons include Eugene Onegin at the Philharmonie de Paris, Ford in Falstaff at the Polish National Opera, Gerhard in Trans-Maghreb at the Bregenzer Festspiele, Valentin in Faust in Poznań and Katowice, Robert in Iolanta at the Estonian National Opera, Rodrigo Posa in Don Carlo at Wrocław Opera, and returns as Germont, Iago, Scarpia, Escamillo, Miecznik, and Amonasro in major Polish opera houses.

Previous highlights include War and Peace at the Bayerische Staatsoper; Yeletsky in The Queen of Spades, Germont, and Eugene Onegin at the Bolshoi Theatre; as well as Don Giovanni, Figaro, Silvio, Miller, Albert, Sharpless, Belcore, and Rodrigo Posa with Opera Nova, Opera Krakowska, Teatr Wielki w Poznaniu, the Polish National Opera, and the Silesian Opera.

A regular guest at major European festivals, he has appeared at the Bregenzer Festspiele, the Kraków Opera Summer Festival, the Bydgoszcz Opera Festival, and the Wrocław Contemporary Opera Festival.
